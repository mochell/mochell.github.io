### September 2026 — Welcome, Bernard!

We are excited to welcome [Bernard Akaawase](https://akaawase-bernard.github.io) to the group as a Postdoctoral Investigator at WHOI. Bernard will be working at the intersection of observations and models to study how surface waves shape small-scale air-sea interaction and offshore wind resources along the New England coast. We look forward to the exciting science ahead!
